About Me

I'm a data engineer with 7+ years of experience building production data platforms across manufacturing, financial services, and retail. My strength is in building dimensional models, pipelines that serve ML, and reliable data infrastructure.

I'm based in Austin, TX, where I build data platforms and agentic AI at Dell Technologies. I also run Asios, a small company building private communication and AI products. Asios Rooms and Asios Standup are live, and all three Asios apps are in development for Android. And I'm an artist.
