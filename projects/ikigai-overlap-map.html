Four circles, your examples

Map where your reasons overlap.

Add small, real examples and tag the circles each one touches. They land in the overlaps, so you can see where things cluster, then turn one into a two-week experiment. No score, no verdict.

A note on context: Ikigai has varied meanings in Japanese life and culture. The four-circle diagram is a popular modern adaptation that circulates mostly outside Japan, not a traditional or validated model. This tool uses it as a visual reflection aid only: an empty centre is normal, and nothing here measures purpose or suggests a career formula.
