Coverage and provenance

  • Tour dates were extracted from public Wikipedia schedule tables using the Wikimedia API and normalized as factual event records. The date dataset is attributed to List of BTS live performances and linked tour pages under CC BY-SA 4.0. Snapshot: October 12, 2026.
  • Ticket counts and grosses below come from publicly accessible Pollstar and Billboard reporting. We did not scrape Pollstar's subscriber-only database. Some records are venue engagements or reporting windows, not single-show figures.
  • “Sold out” is not a ticket count. Attendance is not automatically equal to tickets sold. Online viewers and cinema audiences are not mixed with in-person tickets.
  • This is a sourced research index, not an official BTS/HYBE product. No resale transaction data, private purchase data, logos, images, or ticketing page layouts are included.

Known sales-data gap: complete per-date box-office reporting for every tour, region, and year is not publicly available in the sources reviewed. The app exposes which tours have sourced ticket counts and which have no located public report.

Unofficial factual index. Tour schedule facts are a historical snapshot; check artist, venue, and promoter notices for current changes.
