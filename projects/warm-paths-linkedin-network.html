How the ranking works, and what it cannot know

Each contact gets a transparent score from only what is in your Connections file: a match to a target company (+40), a role keyword in their title (+20), recruiter or talent titles (+15), director, VP, head, chief, or founder titles (+10), manager or lead titles (+6), an industry or topic word in company or title (+8), connected within the last 12 months (+8) or earlier (+4), and a shared email address (+3). Tiers are only ranges of that score. It cannot tell who is hiring, how well you know someone, or whether they will reply, and job titles in the export may be out of date.

Be kind with other people's inboxes: a few specific, honest requests beat a volume blast. Respect LinkedIn's terms and each person's answer.
