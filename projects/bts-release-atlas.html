Completeness rule

Scope includes distinct BTS-credited audio release packages available by the cutoff: Korean/global albums, EPs, single albums, repackages, anthologies, soundtrack and live albums; Japanese albums, compilations, and physical singles; standalone group originals; and co-credited releases. Member-solo catalogs, video-only products, retailer packaging variants, clean edits, and identical-audio reprints are excluded. Remixes and edition deltas are attached to their parent release rather than inflated into separate albums.

No lyrics, audio, artwork, or photography are included. Metadata was cross-checked against BIGHIT/Weverse notices, BTS Japan and label catalogs, and major platform metadata; territory-specific editions can vary.
