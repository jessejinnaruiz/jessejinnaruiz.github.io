California residents: start with DROP

California's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform lets you send one deletion request to over 600 registered data brokers, for free. It launched January 1, 2026. Brokers had to begin processing requests on August 1, 2026 and must check for new ones at least every 45 days, so status updates can take up to 90 days. Sources and limits

DROP dates and eligibility come from the California Privacy Protection Agency's pages below. The letter templates cite the California Consumer Privacy Act, Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, and Colorado Privacy Act by name; confirm current text and how they apply to a given company with the state's attorney general or a qualified adviser. Some brokers fall outside these laws, and credit bureaus are covered by separate rules.
